Civils Avoidance Guide

Field-led guidance to reduce unnecessary civils by completing the right checks, using the correct tools and capturing clear evidence before escalation.

Strong Civils Rationale

  • All recovery options attempted
  • Clear identified blockage point
  • Evidence captured (photos + notes)
  • Correct tools used first

Weak Civils Rationale

  • No recovery attempts made
  • Poor notes ("blocked duct")
  • No clear failure location
  • Escalation without evidence

Pre-booking Checks

  • Confirm build type
  • Check route & records
  • Attempt recovery
  • Capture evidence

Why It Matters

  • Reduces cost
  • Improves customer experience
  • Faster resolutions
  • Consistent decisions
